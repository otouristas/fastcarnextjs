# FMR Naxos editorial briefs

These briefs define intent and evidence requirements. They are not publishable copy.

## Core commercial cluster

- Homepage: own the broad “Naxos rent a car / ενοικίαση αυτοκινήτου Νάξος” intent without a competing landing page.
- Fleet: help visitors choose by group size, transmission, luggage and route.
- Pricing and insurance: explain only owner-verified prices, inclusions, deposits, excess and exclusions.
- Airport, port and Chora: describe the real handover process, meeting point, timing and any fee.

## Vehicle collections

- Automatic cars.
- Family and seven-seat cars.
- SUV and 4×4 cars.

Each collection must map to current inventory and a real booking filter. Avoid implying that a specific model is guaranteed unless the booking contract guarantees it.

## Eight guide pillars

1. Do you need a car in Naxos?
2. Driving rules, licences and IDP requirements.
3. Parking in Chora, beaches and villages.
4. Three-, five- and seven-day road-trip itineraries.
5. Naxos beaches by car, with verified road and parking conditions.
6. Mountain villages driving route.
7. Airport versus port arrival and pickup planning.
8. Rental prices, insurance, deposits and payment checklist.

Government and statutory sources are mandatory for legal or road-safety claims. Owner evidence is mandatory for policies, availability, timing and price claims.
