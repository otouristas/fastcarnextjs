# Business proof pack

Status: owner evidence required before the affected copy, metadata, schema, navigation, or sitemap entry can be published.

The typed registry is `src/content/business-facts.ts`. A repository value is not evidence by itself. Add a durable source reference, owner reviewer, and review date before changing a fact to `verified`.

| Fact group | Evidence required | Current publishing rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Legal identity and licence | Registration certificate, legal trading name, licence number and issuing authority | Withheld |
| NAP and coordinates | Current business document plus owner-confirmed map pin | Address and map schema withheld |
| Seasonal hours | Owner-approved operating calendar | Hours schema withheld |
| Delivery areas and fees | Current written delivery policy | Location pages withheld |
| Fleet and collections | Current inventory export, model/category mapping and booking-engine availability | Only cars and existing vehicle records shown; no live-availability claim |
| Prices and inclusions | Current rate table, taxes, mileage, extras and seasonal dates | Pricing page withheld |
| Deposits and payment | Current contract and payment-provider rules | Withheld |
| Insurance and excess | Current policy wording, exclusions and excess by product | Insurance page withheld |
| Cancellation | Current customer contract and refund timing | Withheld |
| Driver age and licence | Current contract plus authoritative legal sources | Withheld |
| Ferry policy | Current written permission and insurer conditions | Withheld |
| Owners, founding date and service model | Owner attestation and suitable first-party evidence | About page withheld |
| Google profile and reviews | Exact GBP URL and signed-in profile evidence | Rating/review claims withheld |

## Approval record

For every fact promoted to `verified`, record:

- Evidence file or authoritative URL.
- Owner reviewer’s full name.
- Review date.
- Exact approved wording and locales it covers.
- Expiry or next-review date where the fact can change.

Do not infer one policy from another. In particular, booking-engine options do not prove availability, delivery fees, deposit rules, insurance coverage, or cancellation terms.
