# 90-day measurement plan

Baseline date: 2026-07-28. See `search-console-baseline-2026-07-28.md`.

## Release gates

- Deploy and verify the canonical production responses.
- Confirm `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, canonicals and reciprocal hreflang publicly.
- Resubmit the sitemap after the production URL returns a valid sitemap.
- Request indexing only for the principal approved pages in each wave.

## Day 30, 60 and 90 review

Record by query, page, locale, country and device:

- Indexed and excluded URLs.
- Greek non-branded clicks, impressions, CTR and average position.
- Homepage and principal-cluster query movement.
- Consented booking, WhatsApp, phone, email and language-switch actions.
- Google Business Profile website, booking, call and direction interactions.
- Pages with high impressions and weak CTR.
- Cannibalising or untranslated results.

Compare with the fixed 2026-06-14 to 2026-07-26 baseline. Do not interpret ranking volatility as proof of a content win or loss without checking indexing, query mix and seasonality.

## Content cadence

After day 90, prepare two native-reviewed guide additions or material updates per month. Choose them from Search Console evidence and recurring customer questions. Every item must pass editorial validation and owner fact approval before publication.
